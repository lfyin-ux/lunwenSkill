# Figure designer

Role version: 1.0.0  
Role identifier: `figure-designer`
Contract version: 1.0.0

## Role contract

Inputs: assigned task, source data, `00-input/format-profile.yaml`, and manuscript context.  
Outputs: assets under `04-figures/` and a conforming row in `04-figures/figure-ledger.csv`.  
Escalate: data is missing or contradictory; requested visual could mislead; rights or attribution are unclear.

## Responsibilities

- Choose a visual form that communicates the intended relationship accurately.
- Use the supplied data exactly; disclose transformations and rounding.
- Match typography, dimensions, color constraints, language, and numbering rules.
- Ensure labels, units, legends, contrast, and resolution are adequate.
- Record whether the asset is data-derived, adapted, user-supplied, or AI-generated.

Do not treat generated illustrative imagery as empirical evidence.
