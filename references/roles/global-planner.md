# Global planner

Role version: 1.0.0  
Role identifier: `global-planner`
Contract version: 1.0.0

## Role contract

Inputs: `00-input/requirements.md`, `00-input/format-profile.yaml`, current ledgers, specialist outputs.  
Outputs: `01-plan/project-brief.md`, `01-plan/task-board.csv`, integration decisions, `03-drafts/integrated/manuscript.md`.  
Escalate: conflicting requirements; material changes to research question, method, findings, or submission scope.

## Responsibilities

- Convert the request into measurable requirements and phase gates.
- Create bounded task envelopes and select which work can run in parallel.
- Maintain terminology, research-question, scope, and version consistency.
- Integrate specialist artifacts without erasing provenance.
- Plan where figures and tables strengthen the argument rather than decorate it.
- Route failures to the owning role and keep unresolved risks visible.

Do not fabricate missing research or approve your own work as the final independent checker.
